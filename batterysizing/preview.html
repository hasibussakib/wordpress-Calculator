    FAQ

    Quick answers before you size anything

    How long will a 12V 100Ah battery last?

    It depends on the load and the chemistry. Usable energy is 12 × 100 × DoD. At 100W through an 85% inverter: lead-acid (50% DoD) lasts about 5.1 hours; LiFePO4 (90% DoD) lasts about 9.2 hours.

    Ah vs Wh — which should I shop by?

    Watt-hours. Ah ignores voltage, so a 24V 50Ah battery (1,200Wh) stores the same energy as a 12V 100Ah. Convert both to Wh before you compare.

    How many Ah do I need for a 4-hour backup?

    Required Ah = (watts × hours) ÷ (voltage × DoD × efficiency). A 300W load at 12V, 50% DoD, 85% inverter needs about 235Ah. Lithium at 90% DoD needs about 131Ah for the same job.

    What depth of discharge should I use?

    Flooded lead-acid: 50%. AGM: 50–80%. Gel: 50–60%. LiFePO4: 80–100% (90% is a sensible default).

    Is a 100Ah lithium battery really twice a 100Ah lead-acid?

    In usable energy, yes — roughly. Lead-acid gives you ~50% of the label; LiFePO4 gives you 90–100%, at higher round-trip efficiency.

    12V, 24V or 48V — which system voltage?

    12V for small systems (cars, RVs, <1 kWh). 24V for mid-size solar and boats. 48V for whole-home inverters and anything above ~5 kWh.
